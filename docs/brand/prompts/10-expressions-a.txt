The crab is warm sand (#D5B893) with ink outlines, seen from the side or three-quarter view, with two eye stalks topped by round dark eyes with a tiny highlight, ONE big rounded claw on its right and one small claw, and short legs. No mouth, no eyebrows, no blush. Never red, orange or coral. Flat 2D vector, single confident line weight, matte, no gradients, no 3D. Emotion is shown only through the eye stalks, the claw, and how far the crab is out of its shell.
