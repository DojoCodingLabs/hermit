STYLE DIRECTION, "Brutalist manifesto": stark black and white with one signal color (acid lime) used only for tiny accents. Monospace typeface everywhere, raw visible grid lines, oversized type, terminal and manifesto energy, like a hacker zine crossed with Swiss poster design. The crab symbol is built from pixels or a hard-edged geometric construction. Texture panel: photocopied paper grain and halftone. Bold, confrontational, anti-interface.
