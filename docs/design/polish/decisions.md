# Polish round: decisions

Goal of the pass: bring every screen up to the brand's Quiet craft standard: calm, precise, made with care. These are the decisions, with the reason for each. The audit is in `audit.md`; the evidence (before/after, references, clips, contrast) in `index.html`.

The product context comes from the brand book (`docs/brand/BRANDBOOK.md`), `tokens.json` and `AGENTS.md`: Android users in Latin America, es-419 first, an on-device agent whose promise is "Does everything. Tells no one." Personality: discreet, capable, dry, a loyal homebody. Register: product UI, so the tool should disappear into the task; delight is for moments (the crab, a payment going through), not for every screen.

## Type

- **The brand faces are bundled, nothing is fetched.** Static TTFs made by `scripts/fonts/instance_fonts.py` from the google/fonts variable fonts (SHA-256 pinned, OFL-1.1, the license next to the files in `apps/mobile/assets/fonts`), embedded at build time by expo-font's config plugin as Android XML font families, so `fontFamily` plus `fontWeight` pick the right file (Android 10 and up; Hermit's floor is Android 13). About lists the three typefaces; `licenses.test.ts` checks the files, the licenses and the plugin agree.
- **Newsreader: one display cut, optical size 36, weight 450.** Measured on a specimen at 26 to 60 dp on paper and on Deep sea: optical size 72 is too condensed at these sizes, 24 too soft; 450 holds on dark without the heaviness of 500. One file serves screen titles, sheet titles and large amounts.
- **Instrument Sans: Regular for reading, Medium for labels, row titles and buttons.** Two weights a screen. In Deep sea the "strong" weight steps to SemiBold and small text opens by 0.006 em, because light text on a dark ground reads thinner (the only use of 600).
- **JetBrains Mono** only where a person compares characters (a typed address, a hash in details), Regular and Medium.
- **Scale:** 12 caption, 13 footnote, 14 small, 16 body, 19 lead, 26 title, 34 large title, 40 hero, 56 amount. Line heights on the 4 pt grid where they can be (16, 18, 20, 24, 26, 32, 40, 46, 62). Display sizes track tighter (−0.012 to −0.025 em); small sizes open slightly.
- **Tabular figures** for every amount (`fontVariant: tabular-nums`; both faces have `tnum`).
- **Titles never end on one word:** display text and centered text use Android's balanced line breaking (`textBreakStrategy="balanced"`; the preview mirrors it with `text-wrap: balance`).
- **₮ is not in Newsreader or Instrument Sans.** Android draws it from the system font. So "USD₮" is never set in Newsreader: large amounts split into figures (serif) and unit (sans, smaller, beside them), and every sentence with USD₮ is in the sans. Hermit doesn't modify the fonts to add the glyph.
- **Spanish care:** the "…" character (copy already uses it), curly quotes “ ” in both languages from `quoted()` (es-419 uses them; « » and straight quotes go), no em or en dashes (the copy test enforces it), and Intl's narrow no-break space (in "9:05 PM") swapped for a no-break space the faces have.
- **Section labels are sentence case**, a 13 dp footnote in the secondary color, lined up with the rows' text. No ALL CAPS anywhere.

## Surfaces and depth

- **Four layers from the tokens.** Light: canvas = paper `#F5EDE1`; raised (groups, cards) = paper 60% toward white `#FBF8F3`; overlay (sheets) = 70% `#FCFAF6`; sunken (fields, tracks, the segmented track) = paper halfway to shell `#F0E5D6`. Deep sea: canvas abyss, raised trench, overlay trench 30% toward current, sunken halfway from abyss to trench. Raised is lighter than the canvas in both themes, so depth reads the same way.
- **Hairlines, not borders.** Separators are `StyleSheet.hairlineWidth` in ink at 12% (foam at 10% in Deep sea), inset past a row's leading tile. Stone 1px outlines are gone.
- **Soft tinted shadows** (`boxShadow`, new architecture): raised groups `0 1 2` and `0 4 14` at 5% ink; sheets `0 −10 40` at 14%. Deep sea uses its lighter surfaces instead and only deepens sheet shadows.
- **Tints are mixes of a surface and an accent**: the accent's tint (tonal buttons, tiles) is 10% toward sea glass deep from the canvas; amber and coral tints are 8% and 7% from the raised surface, which keeps their own color as text at 4.5:1 or better. Every pair is checked in `a11y-scan.test.ts`.
- **The accent is rare.** Sea glass deep fills one primary button a screen, the Hermit bubble, the meter and the tiles' icons; amber only for "needs you"; coral only for blocked and destructive.

## Shapes and spacing

- **One radius family: 10, 14, 20, 28, full.** 10 icon tiles; 14 fields, quotes and panels inside a surface; 20 groups, cards and bubbles; 28 sheets; full for buttons, badges, avatars and the composer.
- **A 4 pt grid**, a 20 dp gutter on every screen, 32 between sections, 8 from a label to its group, rows at least 56 dp with 12 vertical padding.

## Components

- **Buttons:** one filled primary per screen; a tonal secondary (the accent's tint, no outline); a text tertiary. 52 dp tall (48 for small and text buttons), full radius. They sink to 97% and dim to 88% on press, on a firm spring. Disabled is the sunken fill with soft text, so it reads as waiting rather than faded. Amber and coral keep filled versions for "needs you" and destructive confirms, and a tonal coral for destructive secondaries.
- **Lists:** grouped inset lists on the raised surface, radius 20, leading icons in tinted squircle tiles (32 dp, radius 10), hairlines inset past the tile, chevrons only on rows that open something, a pressed row darkens by 6% ink.
- **Badges:** small tonal labels (24 dp, text only), at most one a row and only when they carry information: "De un mensaje" on a memory, an unknown sender on a transfer, a connector Hermit drafted, a briefing suggestion made after reading messages. Phase 2 removed the rest: Activity's "Hecho" on every row, the credential sheet's and the fill screen's login source, a memory's "Guardado por ti", the import's file format, the web task's site; each is now a quiet line, or nothing.
- **Segmented control** in place of radio cards for two or three short options: a sunken track and a raised thumb that slides. Longer choices are a list with a check on the chosen row.
- **Addresses (address poisoning):** a counterparty with a saved name shows the name, and may add the address cut in the middle as secondary text. Any unnamed or unknown-sender address shows whole: mono, never cut or ellipsized, so a look-alike that shares the first and last characters still reads differently. A whole address reads in groups of four after the 0x (`0xdbF0 3B40 7c01 … C6FB`, `groupAddress` through `WholeAddress`), the way hardware wallets show one for checking: a line breaks only between groups, never leaving a lone character. The grouping is display only: the text isn't selectable, and copying (receive's Copy) uses the address itself. The same display is on the history, the latest movements, the chat's receipt, saved payees, receive and the payment sheet's address row. That holds in the history, the wallet's latest movements, the payment sheet (its title says "¿Enviar a esta dirección?" and the row shows the address whole), the chat's receipt and Hermit's own replies. Wherever a counterparty address can be copied, the whole address is next to the copy action. Only the user's own address on the wallet home stays cut, with copy; Receive shows it whole. `sheetRecipient`, `transferParty` and `ownAddress` hold the rule, with tests for the 42 characters, the saved-payee cut and a look-alike pair.
- **Dates** are relative (`when()`): "Ahora", "Hace 5 min", "Hoy, 9:05 a.m.", "Ayer, 7:10 p.m.", the whole weekday capitalized within a week ("Martes, 8:00 a.m."; "mar" also reads as March), then the date. A time still to come (`ahead()`, reminders) says "Hoy", "Mañana", the weekday, then "24 nov, 10:49 p.m.". A detail screen (an activity entry, a memory) keeps the full date and time.
- **Icons:** Lucide, stroke 1.75, 20 dp in rows, buttons and notes, 24 dp in the app bar; nothing smaller. Badges carry no icon.
- **Fields:** filled with the sunken color, radius 14, a 1.5 dp ring in the accent while focused and coral when invalid; the label above, never a placeholder as the label.
- **One header, everywhere:** `Page`. A 56 dp bar with the back button; the large Newsreader title scrolls with the content and the bar picks up a small copy of it, with a hairline, once it has gone by. An intro sentence is the page's subtitle. Only immersive screens keep the title small in the bar from the start (a web task's live page, a recording). The stack header is hidden on every route; phase 2 moved the last three off it (the page reader, the activity entry, the web tasks screen) and put Delete everything's title on the page in every state.
- **Long lists build only what's on screen.** `Page` has a list mode: a FlatList whose cells draw the Group look one by one (the first and last carry the rounded corners, the others a hairline at the text's inset; each draws the raised shadow and its frame clips it where cells meet, so the stack reads as one card). The vault (an import can bring thousands), Activity, memories and messages use it; older memories load as the list nears its end.
- **Hashes on the activity entry** read in groups of eight (`groupHex`), display only, with a copy button beside each that copies the value itself (a plain clip) and turns into a check for two seconds.
- **Counts say one right:** `tCount` picks a `.one` form when the copy has it ("1 vez", "1 entrada firmada"), so nothing reads "1 veces" or "paso(s)".
- **One text column.** Section labels, section intros, notes, field labels, hints and typed values all start 16 dp in, where the rows' text starts, so a screen reads down one edge. The page title and its subtitle sit on the gutter.
- **A title and its fact (`TitleLine`):** a row's time or amount sits on the title's baseline at its end; from 130% text size it moves under the title, so neither is squeezed to a word per line.
- **No outlined cards.** Card lost its hairline variant; every panel is a surface (raised, or a tint for amber, coral and Hermit). Text in coral and amber passes on the raised surface too.

## Motion

- **Tokens in `apps/mobile/src/motion.ts`,** plain numbers so the mascot branch reads the same ones: durations 90 (press), 160 (a swap), 240 (a state change), 360 (content arriving), 420 (a sheet); curves as cubic Béziers (standard ease-out quint, enter ease-out expo, exit ease-in cubic, crossfade); springs for press, sheet and small things, all damped to settle without a wobble; distances of 10 and 6 dp.
- **Animated on the native driver,** no new library: transform and opacity only, so it runs off the JavaScript thread. Layout changes (a row folding, a message arriving) use LayoutAnimation, which Fabric supports. This round adds no motion library: the screens need no gestures or values computed per frame on the UI thread. (The mascot, from master's mascot v2, brings react-native-reanimated for its own rig; its fades read the same tokens.)
- **What moves:** buttons sink on press; sheets rise on a spring while the backdrop fades; a screen's first block and every new chat message rise 10 dp and fade in; the chat's status word crossfades; steps fold and unfold; the segmented thumb, the switch's knob and the cap meter spring into place.
- **Reduce motion and Quiet mode** (`useAppearance().motion`) stop every movement: things land in place, a press still dims.
- **Haptics with no permission.** expo-haptics and React Native's Vibration need the VIBRATE permission, so phase 2 adds a tiny local Expo module, `apps/mobile/modules/hermit-haptics`: one Kotlin function that calls `View.performHapticFeedback` on the current activity's root view (CONFIRM and REJECT on Android 11 and up, else the closest constant; CLOCK_TICK for small things). No permission in its manifest (a test checks it), no Vibrator. `haptic()` never throws and does nothing in the preview. Used for: the approve after the fingerprint signs (confirm), Deny (reject), a primary button, a switch and the segmented control (tick). It honours the phone's own touch feedback setting.

## Long answers in the chat (Q155)

Design details, logged as Q155, which sets the behavior.

- **A long answer is drawn as structure, never as markdown.** `replyBlocks` (`packages/runtime/src/reply-blocks.ts`) reads the reply's plain lines: a short line ending in a colon (or a `#` or bold line) is a heading in the body's strong weight, `1.` or `1)` lines are numbered steps with the number in tabular strong figures, `-`, `*`, `•` and `·` lines are bullets drawn as the body's own `•`. The `#`, `**`, ticks, rules, quotes and table bars never show. One level of indent is kept. A plain reply is still one text, exactly as before.
- **Hanging indents that hold at 200%.** The marker column grows with the phone's font size (22 dp times the scale, up to twice), so a wrapped step lines up under its own words, not under its number.
- **A structured or long reply takes 92% of the row** instead of 80%, so a recipe reads as a recipe and lines don't wrap a word at a time at large text. Short replies keep the 80% bubble.
- **No links from model text.** A markdown link keeps its words and its address as plain text; nothing in a reply is tappable (Text detects no links unless asked, and none is).
- **The cut line** sits under the bubble, in the quiet style of the thread's notes: the pause icon and "Me detuve aquí." (or "Lo detuviste aquí." after a stop) in the secondary color, and Seguir as a small tonal button with the play icon, 48 dp, its own TalkBack label ("Seguir con la respuesta"). When Seguir finds nothing new (Q173), the same quiet line takes the check icon and "No encontré nada más que agregar.", with no button: the reply is whole. Only the latest reply offers Seguir; an older cut reply keeps its line. While Seguir writes, the line is a spinner and "Siguiendo…", and the composer's Stop stops it.
- **The list follows the newest words** while the user is at the end of the chat, so a long answer doesn't stream below the fold; once they scroll up to read, it stays where they are, and their own next message brings it back down.
- **TalkBack** hears the structure as sentences (headings and items as sentences, steps with their number, no list signs read out). A long reply reads its opening, about 300 characters, then "La respuesta completa está en el chat."; every heading, step and item is its own stop to swipe through, and headings are headers. A cut reply is followed by "Me detuve aquí. Toca Seguir para el resto."; after Seguir only the new part is read.

## Phone checks (native code, checked on a phone)

1. The fonts: after `npx expo prebuild`, `android/app/src/main/res/font/` holds the six TTFs and the three `xml_*.xml` families, and `MainApplication` registers Newsreader, InstrumentSans and JetBrainsMono with `ReactFontManager.addCustomFont`. On the phone, titles are Newsreader (not the system serif), UI text is Instrument Sans in two weights (Medium for labels, SemiBold in Deep sea), addresses are JetBrains Mono, and no text is faux-bold.

3. Tabular figures: the balance and the amounts don't shift width while the balance loads; the history's amounts line up in a column.

On the long lists (the vault, Activity, memories, messages), the cell-by-cell shadow shows no seam between rows while scrolling, and a tap on the search field above the first row still lands.

6. Motion on the native driver: press feedback on buttons, the sheet's spring and backdrop, the first block's rise, the status crossfade, the segmented thumb and the switch knob at 60 fps; LayoutAnimation easing the chat when a message is sent and when steps fold (Fabric); the large title's copy fading into the bar on list pages too.
